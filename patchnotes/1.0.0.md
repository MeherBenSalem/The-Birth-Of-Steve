# Remnants: Roguelite Dungeons 1.0.0 — Remember or Forget

The Birth of Steve becomes **Remnants: Roguelite Dungeons**. The authored campaign stays as its prologue, and the Fractured Archive becomes a repeatable endgame: pick your difficulty, take your party in, and decide at every gateway whether to bank what you carry or risk it all for more. Every number below is a default from the new `config/tbos-expeditions.json`.

## Memory Keys and Attunement

- **Memory Key** (`tbos:memory_key`): four Amethyst Shards in the corners, four Gold Ingots on the edges, an Ender Pearl in the centre. A crafted key is tier 1. Keys go up to tier 20.
- Use a key on a **Waystone** or the **Curator Gateway** to open the **Attunement** screen: the tier, the Distortions, who is coming, the reward factor and the Relic odds. **Begin** is checked again by the server before the run starts.
- Each tier makes every enemy in the run stronger: **health ×(1 + 0.10 per tier above 1)** and **damage ×(1 + 0.05 per tier above 1)**, so ×2.9 health and ×1.95 damage at tier 20.

## Distortions

Toggle them on a key at Attunement. Each adds one **Heat**, and rewards scale with **Fracture** (tier + Heat).

| Distortion | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| **Fortified** | Enemies other than wardens and floor bosses have 30% more health |
| **Tyrannical** | Wardens and floor bosses have 40% more health and deal 15% more damage |
| **Bolstering** | When an enemy falls, allies within 10 blocks gain Strength for 10 s, stacking to Strength III |
| **Volatile** | Fallen enemies burst after 1.5 s for 4 + tier/4 damage within 3 blocks. A hiss and sparks warn you, and it never breaks blocks |
| **Haunted** | Every 30 s of combat a Shade (a half-health Parallax Wraith) slips in beside one of you |
| **Frailty** | One fewer shared revive |
| **Hourglass** | An 8-minute timer per floor. Beat the boss in time for +1 key tier and 25% more Renown |
| **Amnesia** | Living Memories drafts offer two choices instead of three |

## Remember or Forget

- Every cleared room adds **15 × R** Renown to your personal **Recollection**, a lesser warden **40 × R**, and a floor boss **120 × R** plus a Relic. R = 1 + 0.12 × (tier − 1) + 0.15 × Heat.
- At each floor's gateway, choose:
  - **Remember** banks the Recollection multiplied by **Momentum**, and gives you a Memory Key of tier + floors cleared (up to 20, +1 for every Hourglass you beat). With RPG Attribute System installed you also gain Valor equal to a quarter of the banked Renown.
  - **Forget** is a vote to go deeper. When everyone still in the run has voted, the next floor opens and Momentum rises by 0.25 (it starts at ×1.0).
- **Failure** (the shared revives run out, or the run is abandoned) loses every unbanked Recollection, and the key's holder gets back a key one tier lower.
- The choice opens on the gateway by itself. Press **N** or use `/expedition choose` to bring it back.

## Recollections

Use a Memory Key anywhere inside a vanilla or modded structure to open an Expedition seeded from that place and named after it, for example *Recollection of the Trial Chambers*. Each structure can be recollected once per player. Add structures to `#tbos:unrecollectable` to exclude them.

## The Archive Codex

Open it with **N** (rebindable) or `/codex`. Progress is per player.

- **Renown levels 1 to 30.** Level L to L+1 costs 150 + 50 × L Renown, and every level grants one **Insight**.
- **Sixteen Insights**, from Steady Hand and Deep Recall to Keymaker, Party Bond and Last Stand. Respec is free outside a run.
- **Bestiary**: every Archive creature you have slain, with kills, its home floor and a line of lore. Creatures you have not slain stay sealed.
- **Records**: your personal bests and the server's **Hall of Records**, the top ten by highest tier Remembered, then deepest floor.
- **Weekly Recollection**: one key a week for every player on the server, tier 6 to 10 with two fixed Distortions, derived from the world seed and the week (weeks start Monday 00:00 UTC). The deepest floor is ranked.

## Relics

- Floor bosses drop **Relics**: vanilla swords, axes, bows, crossbows and armor with a rarity, rolled affixes, a name and lore. Iron up to tier 6, diamond up to 13, netherite beyond. Item level is 10 + 5 × tier.
- Six rarities: Common (1 affix), Uncommon (2), Rare (2), Epic (3), Legendary (4) and **Remnant** (4 plus a signature, from tier 10).
- Seven weapon affixes, eight armor affixes and four Remnant signatures: Last Hour, Fracture Step, Choir's Echo and Yesterglass.
- Higher tiers and the Keen Eye Insight shift the odds towards the rarer grades.
- Relics are revealed card by card when you Remember, each with its own chime. A Rare or better find raises a beam of its colour over you, and an Epic or better one is announced to your party.
- Relics stay ordinary vanilla items, so enchanting, anvils, Mending and inventory mods all keep working.

## Parties

- `/party create`, `invite <player>`, `accept`, `decline`, `leave`, `kick <player>`, `disband` and `list`. No operator permission needed.
- Up to 4 players. Invites expire after 60 s, and parties survive restarts.
- Party members within 24 blocks of the key holder join the Expedition.
- **Party frames** on the left of the screen show each member's health, absorption, and whether they are in the Archive.

## Also new

- **Expedition HUD**: tier, Distortions, banked Renown, Relics, Momentum and the Hourglass, above the hotbar while you are in a run.
- **Fourteen advancements** in a new Expeditions tab.
- **Second Draft** works from the Memory screen's **Rewrite Draft** button or `/expedition reroll`.
- 27 new sound effects, a new GUI icon set and the new mod logo.
- **Remnants family**: Archive creatures are in `#remnant_mobs:never_leveled`, so Remnants: RPG Mobs leaves their strength to the Fracture Tier.

## Fixes

- On Fabric 1.20.1 and 1.21.1, the Memory loadout and Memory slot keys were never registered, so they did not appear in Controls and did nothing. They work now.
- On 1.21.1 the Waystone recipe failed to load, so Waystones could not be crafted. They can now.
