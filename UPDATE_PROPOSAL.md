# Living Memories

Fight alongside the memory of your own actions, shaping echoes through artifacts and reconstructed terrain.

The signature is composable combat memory: action → delivery → modifier → impact → aftermath → bounded echo. Six discovered actives fill three freely equipped slots; twelve passive discoveries alter behavior without occupying ordinary inventory slots. Powers apply only during the Archive run.

## Experience

Floor one establishes a build, floor two introduces temptation and socket interactions, floor three delivers a memory climax and extraction/endless choice. Target 30–45 minutes, subject to measured playtesting. Preserve the authored adventure, existing saves and all shipped targets.

Actives: Echo Lance, Recall, Parallax Step, Resonant Guard, Reconstruct, Memory Well. Passives: Split Prism, Seeking Glass, Ember Script, Storm Filament, Shatter Seal, Piercing Index, Returning Thread, Delayed Ink, Resonant Nail, Memory Wick, Ward Fragment, Mason's Remnant.

Three-choice personal drafts begin at the first combat clear. Two active discoveries are guaranteed during floor one. Overwrite strengthens an owned artifact in exchange for an explicit temporary personal drawback and optional encounter; it never spends shared revives.

Prisms redirect, conduits transfer and bulwarks reconstruct cover. A sealed socket and concealed circuit reward experimentation. Secrets remain optional and cannot bypass the mandatory gate.

Bosses retain native identities, with readable danger footprints and recovery windows. The third-floor treatment teaches a socket pattern, combines it with native attacks, then alternates both. Health tuning must be measured, not presented as completed balance work.

## Visual and audio identity

Pale cyan memories, amber rewards, violet instability. Thin directional trails and brief impacts establish causality. Enemy warnings outrank friendly spectacle. Short pitched sounds distinguish discoveries, guard success, memory release and room clear; no repeated loud wall of sound.

## Five trailer moments

1. Recall releases a splitting, seeking elemental volley.
2. A reconstructed prism redirects a shot through a conduit.
3. Overwrite turns a weak build into a dangerous temptation.
4. An experimental shot reveals a concealed chamber.
5. Four complementary builds defeat the climax with readable telegraphs.
